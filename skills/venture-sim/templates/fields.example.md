## Wholesale sourdough for cafés

You are the **bakery supplier**.

The business: bake (or co-pack with a partner bakery) sourdough and pastries, and supply them to cafés in Valencia every morning using her uncle's van.

Find out with data:
- Café wholesale prices vs. retail prices.
- Ingredient and co-packing costs per loaf.
- How many cafés switch supplier, and why.
- Health registration for selling food wholesale.
- Delivery cost per round.

Known objection to answer: "Big industrial bakeries undercut everyone on price."

## Instagram baking workshops

You are the **workshop host**.

The business: paid weekend sourdough and pastry workshops (8–12 people) in a rented kitchen, sold through her Instagram, then online classes.

Find out with data:
- Workshop prices in Valencia.
- Kitchen rental per day and ingredient cost per seat.
- Fill rates for new hosts.
- What converts followers into paying seats.

Known objection to answer: "4,000 followers is too small to fill classes every month."
