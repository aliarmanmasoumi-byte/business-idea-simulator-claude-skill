# Profile (fictional example — replace everything)

- Sara, 27, lives in Valencia, Spain.
- Works part-time in a bakery.
- Spanish native, English good.

## Goal
€1,000/month NET profit within a year, in something that can grow to €10k+/month later.

## Time
15 h/week now. Up to 40 h/week once it earns €1,000/month.

- Good at Instagram: her personal account has 4,000 followers.

- Dropshipping, 2025: ads cost more than the profit. She stopped after €600.

## Dislikes and constraints
- Dislikes cold calling.
- **Hard constraint:** no business that needs her at night (she has a child).
